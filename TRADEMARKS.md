# Trademark & Compatibility Policy

This document describes the trademark guidelines for the **Fasti** name, logo, and the **Scrobble.dev Compatible** conformance mark.

---

## 1. The Fasti Name and Logo

The name **Fasti** and the Fasti logomarks (including the Open Folio symbol and wordmark) identify official releases of the Fasti software.

### Permitted Uses
* **Factual references:** You may use the name "Fasti" to truthfully state that your software connects to, imports from, or interoperates with Fasti (e.g., *"Plex plugin for Fasti"*).
* **Community articles and reviews:** You may use the Fasti name in educational tutorials, blog posts, and reviews.

### Uses Requiring Prior Written Permission
* You may **not** use "Fasti" as part of your company name, product name, or top-level domain in a manner that implies official sponsorship, affiliation, or endorsement.
* You may **not** use the official Fasti logo as the primary icon or avatar for a commercial product or service.

---

## 2. Fork Policy

We celebrate the freedom to fork granted by the [AGPL-3.0 Licence](LICENSE). However, trademark law is separate from copyright licensing:

* If you distribute a modified or custom build of Fasti, you **must remove or replace the official Fasti logos and branding** unless your modifications are trivial packaging fixes for an open-source distribution.
* You may describe your project factually: *"Based on the open-source Fasti codebase"*, but not *"Official Fasti Enterprise Edition"*.

---

## 3. Scrobble.dev Compatibility Marks

[Scrobble.dev](https://scrobble.dev) maintains independent activity profile specifications.

* **Factual Compatibility:** Any project that implements a published Scrobble.dev profile may state:  
  `Implements Scrobble.dev Activity Profile 0.1` or `Compatible with Scrobble.dev Activity Profile 0.1`.
* **Official Compatibility Badge:** The official `Scrobble.dev Compatible` badge is reserved for projects whose implementations pass the published automated conformance test suite.
* Earning the compatibility mark certifies technical conformance; it does **not** imply endorsement, partnership, or warranty by Fasti or Scrobble.dev.
